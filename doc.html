Cheerio empowers 5,000+ businesses to scale over WhatsApp, SMS, and AI Voice. However, non-technical users regularly get stuck on Meta API setup, template formatting, chatbot flows, and webhooks—generating ~12,000 tickets/month. This proposal presents Cheerio Pilot: an in-situ diagnostic and 1-click self-healing copilot that achieves 65% autonomous deflection, slashes time-to-first-campaign from 48 hours to under 6 hours, and delivers $83,800 (~₹70 Lakhs) in net annual savings at an AI running cost of just $36/month.

2. Possible Solutions & Prioritization (RICE Framework)

Evaluated 4 core approaches: (1) Static Documentation Library [RICE 50], (2) Generic AI Support Bot [RICE 53], (3) Embedded Diagnostic & Auto-Fix Copilot ("Cheerio Pilot") [RICE 202.5], and (4) Co-Browsing Concierge [RICE 21].

Why Option 3 was picked: Non-technical users do not read long documentation, and generic chatbots cannot access backend state. Option 3 acts directly at the point of failure: it intercepts the error payload, generates a 1-click deterministic fix, and verifies it with Meta Graph API.

3. Chosen Solution: What It Will Do & What It Will NOT Do

✓ What Cheerio Pilot WILL Do

  • Translates cryptic Meta/Shopify errors into plain non-technical English.
  • 1-click auto-repair for template parameters and sample data injection.
  • Visual DAG linter that auto-synthesizes fallback nodes for broken flows.
  • Pre-flight validation on CSV audience lists and country codes (+91).
  • Auto-packages diagnostic dossiers when escalating to human specialists.

× What Cheerio Pilot Will NOT Do

  • Cannot bypass Meta Commerce Policies or legalize prohibited goods.
  • Will never perform irreversible actions (deleting contacts, wiping WABA).
  • Does not hallucinate fake approval timelines or artificial guarantees.
  • Does not replace human account managers for high-touch enterprise contracts.

4. When Does a Human Still Need to Step In?

Strict, non-negotiable escalation triggers:

  • Meta Legal Verification & Green Tick Compliance: Discrepancies between GST certificates and Meta Business Manager require human liaison.
  • Financial & Billing Disputes: Wallet debits, payment gateway 3DS failures, and credit line upgrades.
  • Exhaustion Guardrail (2-Fail Rule): If automated remediation fails twice, execution halts to avoid retry storms.
  • Negative Sentiment / Explicit Request: User requests human assistance or expresses frustration.

Zero-Friction Escalation: The human receives a pre-assembled Diagnostic Dossier (WABA ID, raw payload, steps tried, urgency). The user never has to re-explain anything.

Part 3 — How AI Was Used (AI Usage Diary)

1. AI Tools Utilized

Claude 3.5 Sonnet (system architecture, failure mode taxonomy), OpenAI GPT-4o-mini (token economics, spreadsheet formulas), and Cursor / Antigravity IDE (interactive web prototype coding & verification).

2. Key Prompts: 3 That Worked & 1 That Failed

  • Worked 1 (Domain Roleplay): Framed model as Principal PM at Meta BSP to map error codes (#131030, #133005) into plain-language root causes and deterministic payloads.
  • Worked 2 (Formula Derivation): Demanded exact Google Sheet formula syntax (`=SUM`, `=B5*C5`) for 5,000 businesses and 12,000 monthly tickets at $0.15/$0.60 per 1M tokens.
  • Worked 3 (Interactive Prototype): Scaffolding Tailwind CSS UI with phone preview mockup and live telemetry counters.
  • Failed 1 (The Generic Support Bot): Asked for an open-ended chatbot to "answer all questions". Result was a generic, hallucinating bot that promised impossible feats (guaranteed instant green ticks). Revision: Pivoted to an in-situ diagnostic & 1-click auto-fix engine grounded in backend logs.

3. Hallucinations Caught & Overruled

Caught and removed false promises on Meta approval speed; caught omission of WhatsApp mobile app coexistence constraints (error #133005); overruled AI's suggestion to charge users $0.50 per support conversation (which creates onboarding friction).

4. Manual Thinking Done Without AI

Strategic product positioning ("Solve at point of failure with 1-click fix, not chat"), RICE prioritization trade-offs, hard legal/compliance escalation boundaries, and unit economics sanity checks for Indian support salaries (₹45,000/mo fully loaded).
