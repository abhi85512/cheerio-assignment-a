Part 1 & 3 Product Architecture

Cheerio Pilot: Solution Specification & Operational Blueprint

Full design decisions, scope boundaries, human escalation criteria, and 90-day North Star metric roadmap.

What It Does

  • Auto-translates cryptic Meta/Shopify errors into non-technical explanations
  • 1-click visual auto-fix for WhatsApp template syntax (`{{1}}`, sample values)
  • Pre-flight validation on audience CSV format, country codes, and opt-ins
  • Detects dead-ends & missing branches in chatbot workflows with 1-click patches
  • Packages contextual diagnostic dossier when escalating to human agents

What It Does NOT Do

  • Cannot bypass Meta Commerce Policy bans or override rejected business legality
  • Cannot perform irreversible destructive actions (deleting contacts, wiping numbers)
  • Never hallucinates nonexistent API features or fake approval timelines
  • Does not replace human relationship managers for enterprise SLA contracts

Human Escalation Triggers

  • Meta Official Business Account (Green Tick) and Legal GSTIN validation
  • Billing disputes, unexpected wallet debits, or payment gateway failures
  • User expresses negative sentiment or clicks "Talk to Human Specialist"
  • 2 consecutive failed auto-remediation attempts

Metric 1

Autonomous Ticket Deflection

Target: 65% of incoming L1 setup queries resolved with zero human touch.

Time to First Campaign (TTFC)

Target: Reduce average onboarding time from 48 hours to under 6 hours.

First-Contact Resolution (FCR) & CSAT

Target: >75% FCR on automated sessions; CSAT rating >4.5 / 5.0.
